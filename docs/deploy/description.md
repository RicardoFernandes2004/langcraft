# LangCraft

**Learn a language or pay for it.** LangCraft quizzes you on the items in your inventory and the mobs around you. Get it right and you earn rewards. Get it wrong and you lose items, levels, or worse.

Study **Korean, Japanese, English, German and Spanish**, alone or mixed.

## How it works

Every few minutes, a challenge appears while you play:

- **Speak:** say the item's name into your microphone. Speech recognition runs on your computer, so your voice never leaves it. You can also type the answer.
- **Quiz:** pick the right word from a list.
- **Hold:** find the item and put it in your hand.
- **Mobs:** name a mob. Fail, and it spawns on top of you, from a chicken to a Warden.

## What losing costs you

- A roulette decides how much of the item you lose, up to the whole stack.
- You lose experience levels, and more for each mistake in a row.
- **Daily roulette:** once per game day, a mistake may open a nasty roulette with ambushes, curses and lost stacks.
- **Disaster roulette:** three mistakes in a row. You may lose your armor, your hotbar, half your inventory, or your life.

## What winning gets you

- Experience on every correct answer, doubled when you speak.
- A reward roulette and a shield every 5 correct answers in a row.
- A jackpot every 15: diamonds, totems, netherite and more.
- Titles above your name, from Novice to Language Master, and a ranking on the Tab list.

## Study without the pressure

Press **K** to open your notebook, with spaced repetition, a list of every word you have seen, and alphabet training for Hangul, Hiragana and Katakana. Nothing there can punish you.

## Good to know

- **Works with other mods.** Items and mobs from other mods join the challenges when they have a translation. Use `/lang missing` to fill in the rest yourself.
- **Downloads on first use.** The mod downloads a voice model for each language you choose, from 40 MB to 160 MB. Korean also downloads a Whisper model. Files come from alphacephei.com and huggingface.co and stay in your `config/langcraft` folder.
- **Windows and Linux on x86-64 only.** Voice recognition does not run on macOS or on ARM.
- **Install it on the server too.** Challenges are controlled by the server. In singleplayer, you only need it once.

Use `/lang` in game to see every command.
